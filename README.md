# 📊 Excel Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to identify sales trends, top-performing product categories, outlet performance, location performance, and customer ratings.

An interactive Excel dashboard was created to present important business KPIs and insights in an easy-to-understand format.

* Data Cleaning
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* VLOOKUP / XLOOKUP

* Data Analysis

* Business Insights

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Product Category Performance

* **Fruits & Vegetables** generated the highest sales of approximately **$178.12K**.
* **Snack Foods** followed closely with approximately **$175.43K**.
* **Seafood** had the lowest sales at approximately **$9.08K**.
* Breakfast was also a low-performing category with approximately **$15.60K**.

### 2. Outlet Size Analysis

* **Medium outlets** generated the highest sales at approximately **$507.90K**.
* Small outlets generated approximately **$444.79K**.
* High-size outlets generated approximately **$248.99K**.

### 3. Location Tier Analysis

* **Tier 3 locations** generated the highest sales at approximately **$472.13K**.
* Tier 2 generated approximately **$393.15K**.
* Tier 1 generated approximately **$336.40K**.

### 4. Outlet Type Analysis

* **Supermarket Type1** was the strongest outlet type with approximately **$787.55K** in sales.
* Grocery Stores generated approximately **$151.94K**.
* Supermarket Type2 and Type3 generated approximately **$131.48K** and **$130.71K** respectively.

### 5. Fat Content Analysis

* **Low Fat products** contributed approximately **65%** of total sales.
* Regular products contributed approximately **35%**.

### 6. Establishment Year Analysis

* **2018** was the strongest visible establishment year with approximately **$204.52K** in sales.
* **2011** was the weakest visible year with approximately **$78.13K**.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Focus on high-performing categories such as **Fruits & Vegetables** and **Snack Foods**.
* Review the performance of low-selling categories such as **Seafood** and **Breakfast**.
* Improve productivity and product mix in **High-size outlets**.
* Explore expansion opportunities in **Tier 3 locations**.
* Continue focusing on the **Supermarket Type1** format because it is the strongest sales contributor.
* Maintain strong availability of **Low Fat products** because they contribute the majority of sales.
* Study the successful practices of the **2018 outlet cohort**.
